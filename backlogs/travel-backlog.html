Mega Paper

Ad-hoc, Approval & Booking

Ad-hoc study trips: travel/budget approval paper, soft booking (request submitted), hard booking (itinerary confirmed) and registration.

TRV-202As Travel Desk / FBP, I want to log flight and accommodation quotes against a request (cc FBP Travel), so ACAD can prepare the budget paper.3Manual quote capture + cc

TRV-204As ACAD, I want to put up a Budget Paper within a week of receiving quotes, pre-filled from the quote data, so the approval chain starts promptly.5Pre-filled paper + kickoff
TRV-205As an approver (HOD/ED/etc.), I want to Approve, Amend-and-resubmit, or Reject the budget paper, so the requester knows exactly what to fix.5Multi-tier chain + amend loop
TRV-206As the system, once the budget paper is fully approved, I want to notify the Travel Officer (cc FBP Travel, DTRs, HR) and update the tracking record automatically.5Multi-recipient notify + tracker
TRV-207As an LE, I want to submit the Travel Request Form (soft booking) with officer, division, dates, destination, funding source, class of travel and passport type, so FCM Travel Desk and FBP Travel can act on it.8~20-field form, funding branch
TRV-208As the system, I want to route the Travel Request Form email to the correct mailbox — FCM Travel Desk/FBP Travel, or Academy/DILES for training trips — based on trip type, so requests reach the right owner automatically.5Type-based dynamic routing
TRV-209As an LE, I want to attach the flight itinerary and hotel voucher received from FCM Travel Desk (hard booking) to the request, so the record reflects the confirmed booking.3File upload to record
TRV-210As an LE, I want to submit a Note Verbale request for UNCITRAL registration and forward the generated email template to the right Mission (New York/Vienna) by destination, so the officer can self-register on INDICO.5Destination routing + template
TRV-211As an LE, I want the HOD's approved nomination to auto-populate on the travel insurance and travel request forms, so I don't re-enter approval data.3Cross-form field lookup

PHASE 4

Pre & Post Trip

Checklists and reminders either side of travel — insurance, equipment loans and gifts before; returns, reports and filing after.

IDUser storyPtsSizing note
TRV-401As the system, I want to send a pre-trip checklist notification 3 weeks before travel — insurance, passport release, laptop loan/ITSR, GOMAX device, portable monitors, corporate gifts — so officers complete prep on time.5Scheduled multi-item checklist
TRV-402As an officer/LE, I want to trigger passport release, IT equipment loans and a corporate gifts request as individual pre-trip tasks with owner and deadline, so nothing is missed before departure.5Several parallel task triggers
TRV-403As the system, I want to send a post-trip reminder set — return laptop, declare/return excess gifts, return monitors, submit trip report — on trip completion, so officers close out obligations.5Scheduled multi-item reminders
TRV-404As ACAD/TO, I want to redact the approval paper, register the officer for the course, and notify TO of trip completion (cc FBP, FBP Travel, DTRs, HR) within 3 working days of return, so records and enrolment stay current.5Multi-action close-out + cc
TRV-405As the system, I want to escalate and chase for the trip report if it isn't submitted within 1.5 months of return, so overdue reports don't fall through the cracks.3SLA-based escalation
TRV-406As ACAD/TO, I want a completed trip filed into eFile and IW automatically once the report is in, so the case closes with full documentation.3System-to-system filing
